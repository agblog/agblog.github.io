My Daily Fitness Routine

What I do every single day to stay on track.

Morning Essentials (Every Day)

5:30 AM Wake up and make a fresh banana protein shake with milk. If I want a change, I can mix it up with almonds, oats, pistachios, or any other clean alternative.
6:00 AM Head out for a morning jog. Keep a steady pace for 30 minutes until 6:30 AM to get the heart rate up.

What happens at 6:30 AM through the week:

Monday
Focusing entirely on Chest today
  • Start off strong with 3 sets of classic pushups.
  • Move on to 3 sets of chest presses using the dumbbells.
  • Finish up the chest work with 3 sets using the straight rod/barbell press.
Tuesday
Time to work on the arms after jogging and protein
  • Begin with a few solid sets of bicep curls using dumbbells.
  • Switch over to the straight rod to do heavier bicep sets.
  • Wrap up the session with tricep sets to balance it all out.
Wednesday
Hitting shoulders right after the morning shake
  • Run through standard shoulder workout sets with the dumbbells.
  • Follow that up with overhead shoulder press sets using the rod.
Thursday
Working on the back muscles today
  • Get right into back exercise sets using the dumbbells.
  • Finish the workout with solid back sets using the rods.
Friday
Leg day to finish out the heavy lifting week
  • Get into the leg movements using dumbbells for weight.
  • Transition over to the rod to complete the remaining leg sets.
Saturday
Slowing things down after the jog and protein intake
  • Spend the session doing yoga movements.
  • Deeply stretch out all the muscles used during the week to recover.
Sunday
Rest and Recovery
  • Completely off today. No alarms, no pressure—just letting the body heal up for next week.
